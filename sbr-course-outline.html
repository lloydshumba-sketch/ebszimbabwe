Strategic Business Reporting

A structured financial reporting route covering corporate reporting ethics, IFRS principles, major standards, group accounting and exam-focused revision.

  • Structured teaching sequence
  • Assignment deadlines built into the academic route
  • Revision work designed for exam execution

Academic Structure

A clear route from learning to marked practice and revision.

Learning Pathway

The outline is organised to support disciplined exam preparation.

01

Reporting principles and ethics

The early phase establishes corporate reporting behaviour, the conceptual framework, presentation, disclosure and revenue principles.

02

Core IFRS application

The middle phase develops the major standards, including non-current assets, financial instruments, leases, employee benefits, tax, provisions and fair value.

03

Groups, foreign currency and revision

The final phase builds group accounting, foreign transactions and entities, followed by exam-focused question revision and mock preparation.
