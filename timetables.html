AAA Stream

Advanced Audit and Assurance

AAA demands advanced audit judgment, ethical reasoning, precise risk assessment, and examiner-focused articulation. The rhythm below enforces practical mastery: internalize the principle, apply it to complex scenarios, and refine your written technique.

Class days Monday, Wednesday and Friday
Start time 20:00 CAT
Main student action Prepare scenarios, attempt simulations, and submit written execution.
AAA December 2026 weekly timetable
Day Time Class Purpose Student Preparation
Monday 20:00 CAT Technical instruction: audit principles, ethical frameworks, ISA logic, quality management, and core concept deconstruction. Review the syllabus outline prior to class and prepare targeted questions.
Wednesday 20:00 CAT Scenario analysis: audit risk, business risk, materiality calibration, procedures, and professional script construction. Analyze short scenario extracts and practice translating facts into mark-earning points.
Friday 20:00 CAT Execution and feedback: script evaluation, targeted correction, timing discipline, and revision strategy. Submit required written assignments and review comprehensive corrections prior to the session.
Direct guidance: AAA demands active execution. Prioritize live attendance, draft comprehensive answers, and rigorously refine your narrative style early in the cycle.
SBR Stream

Strategic Business Reporting

SBR requires uncompromising IFRS clarity, sound professional judgment, and the capacity to articulate reporting consequences. This schedule deliberately separates technical acquisition from written application to ensure absolute proficiency.

Class days Tuesday, Thursday and Friday
Start times Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 20:00 CAT
Main student action Internalize IFRS mechanics before class and practice executing clear, professional explanations.
SBR December 2026 weekly timetable
Day Time Class Purpose Student Preparation
Tuesday 20:00 CAT IFRS technical instruction: recognition, measurement, presentation, and comprehensive disclosure principles. Review the designated standard prior to class and isolate any ambiguous technical treatments.
Thursday 20:00 CAT Scenario application: applying IFRS frameworks to complex transactions and articulating the precise reporting impact. Draft concise, professional written explanations, rather than relying solely on numerical calculation.
Friday 20:00 CAT Execution clinic: resolving reporting deficiencies, correcting weak narratives, and elevating professional discussion standards. Submit attempted exam scenarios and clearly identify areas of conceptual friction.
Direct guidance: SBR rewards authoritative explanation. Transcend standard memorization; rigorously practice writing the accounting treatment in commanding, professional language.
Learning Rhythm

The curriculum dictates a deliberate academic rhythm.

The weekly structure is meticulously planned. Candidates progress logically from technical instruction, to practical execution, to rigorous correction and strategic revision.

01

Engage and comprehend

Your primary objective is to attend sessions and definitively master the underlying technical mechanics.

  • Connect to live sessions promptly
  • Compile structured, authoritative notes
  • Interrogate ambiguous concepts immediately
02

Execute and submit

True progression occurs when you draft comprehensive solutions and submit them for rigorous evaluation.

  • Execute exam-standard simulations
  • Submit written responses for evaluation
  • Eradicate identified technical weaknesses
03

Refine and control pace

The final phase demands execution speed, unwavering confidence, strict exam discipline, and the elimination of recurring errors.

  • Systematically review session recordings
  • Execute simulations under strict time parameters
  • Rectify persistent mechanical errors
