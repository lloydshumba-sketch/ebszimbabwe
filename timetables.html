AAA Stream

Advanced Audit and Assurance

AAA is for students who need audit judgement, ethics, risk identification, audit procedures and examiner-style writing. The rhythm below keeps the paper practical: learn the principle, apply it to a scenario, then correct your written approach.

Class days Monday, Wednesday and Friday
Start time 20:00 CAT
Main student action Prepare scenarios, attempt questions and submit written practice.
AAA September 2026 weekly timetable
Day Time Class Purpose Student Preparation
Monday 20:00 CAT Technical teaching: audit principles, ethics, ISA logic, quality management and core topic explanation. Read the topic outline before class and bring questions from the notes.
Wednesday 20:00 CAT Scenario analysis: audit risk, business risk, materiality, procedures and professional answer construction. Attempt short scenario extracts and practise turning facts into mark-earning points.
Friday 20:00 CAT Practice and feedback: marked work, correction of weak scripts, timing discipline and revision control. Submit written practice where required and review corrections before the next class.
Direct guidance: AAA is not passed by listening only. Attend live where possible, write answers, and correct your script style early.
SBR Stream

Strategic Business Reporting

SBR is for students who need IFRS clarity, professional judgement and the ability to explain reporting consequences. The timetable separates technical understanding from application and written discussion.

Class days Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday
Start times Tuesday at 18:30 CAT; Thursday and Saturday at 20:00 CAT
Main student action Revise IFRS areas before class and practise explaining treatments clearly.
SBR September 2026 weekly timetable
Day Time Class Purpose Student Preparation
Tuesday 18:30 CAT IFRS technical class: recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure principles. Read the standard area before class and note the treatment you do not understand.
Thursday 20:00 CAT Scenario application: applying IFRS to transactions and explaining the reporting effect. Practise short written explanations, not only calculations.
Saturday 20:00 CAT Question practice and feedback clinic: correcting weak reporting answers and improving professional discussion. Bring attempted questions and mark where you got stuck.
Direct guidance: SBR rewards clear explanation. Do not only memorise standards; practise writing the accounting treatment in plain, professional language.
ATX Stream

Advanced Taxation UK

ATX is for students who need tax rule discipline, computation accuracy, planning logic and professional advisory writing. The timetable keeps technical work, planning and question correction separate.

Class days Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday
Class window Tuesday 20:00 CAT · Thursday 18:30 CAT · Sunday 20:00 CAT
Main student action Bring computations, tax notes and attempted advisory questions.
ATX September 2026 weekly timetable
Day Time Class Purpose Student Preparation
Tuesday 20:00 CAT Technical tax rules and computation discipline: core rules, reliefs, exemptions and exam traps. Revise the tax area and prepare calculation questions before class.
Thursday 18:30 CAT Tax planning and advisory application: applying rules to client scenarios and recommending action. Prepare written advisory points and identify the tax planning issue in the scenario.
Sunday 20:00 CAT Question practice and correction: timed practice, computation review and advisory style improvement. Bring attempted questions so corrections can be practical and specific.
Direct guidance: ATX needs both numbers and advice. Practise computations, but also learn how to explain tax consequences to a client.
Learning Rhythm

The timetable follows a simple academic rhythm.

The week is not random. Students move from teaching, to practice, to correction and revision.

01

Attend and understand

The first job is to attend class and understand the technical issue properly.

  • Join live classes on time
  • Take structured notes
  • Ask questions early
02

Practise and submit

Real improvement starts when students write answers and submit work for correction.

  • Attempt exam-style questions
  • Submit written practice
  • Correct weak points
03

Revise and control timing

The final stage is about speed, confidence, exam discipline and avoiding repeated mistakes.

  • Review recordings
  • Practise under time pressure
  • Fix recurring errors
