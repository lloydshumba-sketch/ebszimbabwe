Advanced Audit and Assurance

A structured audit route covering professional ethics, quality management, planning, audit evidence, group audits, reporting, sustainability assurance and other professional assignments.

  • Structured teaching sequence
  • Assignment deadlines built into the academic route
  • Revision work designed for exam execution
  • Suitable for working professionals following evening tuition
Academic Structure

A clear route from audit fundamentals to final revision execution.

22 Lectures
5 Assignments
11 Revision Tasks
AAA Professional Paper
Learning Pathway

The outline is organised to build professional audit judgement progressively.

01

Ethics, regulation and professional conduct

The early phase establishes the AAA exam context, money laundering, laws and regulations, ethical principles, fraud and professional behaviour.

02

Planning, evidence and audit execution

The middle phase develops risk assessment, materiality, audit evidence, procedures, using the work of others and group audit considerations.

03

Completion, reporting and specialist assignments

The final phase covers subsequent events, going concern, auditor reporting, governance reporting, sustainability assurance, prospective financial information, forensic audits and due diligence.
