Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for serious ACCA candidates.

The frequently asked questions are written in a direct, student-ready tone so the page feels useful, readable and professional.

Which ACCA papers are offered?

Excel Business School focuses on AAA, SBR and ATX for students who need advanced professional tuition and exam-focused preparation.

Are classes suitable for working professionals?

Yes. The model is built around evening learning, recorded access, WhatsApp support and structured study discipline.

Do students receive marked practice?

Yes. Marked scripts and feedback are central to the learning process because students need to improve how they write, not only what they know.

What makes AAA different from real audit work?

AAA and real-world audit practice are different. AAA requires exam technique, scenario application, professional scepticism and disciplined answer structure. Practical audit experience can help, but students still need to master the ACCA examination approach.

How do I access the timetable or portal?

Use the Timetables and Student Portal links in the navigation menu. Enrolled students receive access guidance after admission confirmation.
